Privacy Policy

Yami Display does not collect, store, or share personal information.

Yami Display operates locally on your Mac. Display settings and preferences are processed and stored on your device.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the Mac App Store and StoreKit. Yami Display does not receive or store your payment information.

If you contact us through the support form, the information you provide, including your email address and message, will only be used to respond to your inquiry.
