Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Adenosine adjusts the appearance of your Mac’s displays locally. We do not receive your screen contents or display preferences. If you choose to contact us, we receive the information you send.

On your Mac

Adenosine uses local display identifiers to remember settings for each screen, including its tint, brightness, and warmth. These preferences stay on your Mac between sessions. The app does not record your screen, read your documents, require an Adenosine account, or include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Purchases and trial access

Apple processes the free seven-day trial and the one-time full-version purchase through the Mac App Store. Adenosine uses Apple’s StoreKit transaction information to check access, calculate the trial end date, and restore purchases. It keeps a trial-verification timestamp in your Mac’s Keychain; this can remain after uninstalling the app. We do not receive your payment card information through Adenosine.

Apple manages its own purchase records under Apple’s privacy policy. Deleting a support message does not delete an App Store transaction or restart a trial.

When you contact support

If you submit the support form, we receive your reply email, message, and any name or details you choose to provide. We use them to respond, troubleshoot your issue, and handle follow-up or privacy requests. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising or mailing lists.

Formspree processes the submission, including spam prevention and delivery. Google (Gmail) hosts our support correspondence. Both services handle the information needed for their respective roles. Sending a message is optional; it is not required to use Adenosine.

Visiting this website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security when it serves a page. We have not added analytics or advertising scripts to this site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and related follow-up, or as required by law. When it is no longer needed, we delete the correspondence we control. Formspree’s submission archive and provider backups may retain copies for their own retention periods.

You can ask us to delete a support conversation or withdraw permission for further follow-up using the support form. Use the same reply email and identify the message so we can locate it. We may ask you to confirm the request by email before deleting it. We will remove the records we control from Gmail and Formspree unless we are legally required to retain them.

For help removing Adenosine’s local settings or trial timestamp, contact us. We cannot remotely access or erase data on your Mac.
