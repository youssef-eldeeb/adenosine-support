Quick answers

Where do I find Adenosine after opening it?

Click its icon in the Mac menu bar. Expand a display’s card to see its controls. Choose a color to tint bright whites; click the selected color again to return to neutral.

Why don’t I see brightness and warmth sliders?

Those sliders appear on external display cards. On a built-in display, use the four tint swatches and adjust brightness with your Mac’s usual controls.

How do I restore the full version?

Sign in to the Mac App Store with the Apple Account used for your purchase. Open Adenosine and choose Restore Purchases on the trial or unlock screen. The seven-day trial does not automatically charge you; unlocking the full version is a separate one-time purchase.
